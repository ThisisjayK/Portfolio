05 · Highlighter wipe

A fill instead of an underline

Hover or tab through. The fill wipes in from the left in 400ms and leaves to the right; the current page keeps it. CSS only: one pseudo-element scaling on x. Candidates: the bottom-left Contact, the letter page cross, the home buttons.

06 · Grid hop, a calmer page change

A mosaic that settles

An alternative to the vapour. The view is cut into 32px cells; each first shows a nearby cell, then snaps into place at its own moment (about half a second in all). The roll box in the corner is chrome: it only twitches, so the frame feels steady.
